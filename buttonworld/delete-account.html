ButtonWorld account deletion

You can request the deletion of your ButtonWorld player account and the game data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email cartographe_games@outlook.com with the subject ButtonWorld account deletion.
  2. Include your in-game username so we can identify the account. Do not send your password.
  3. We may ask for limited information to verify that the request concerns your account.
Request account deletion

What will be deleted

After the request is verified, we will delete the Firebase Authentication account and the associated player profile and game data that can be linked to that account, subject to any data that must be retained for legitimate legal or security reasons.

How long does it take?

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. We will contact you by email if we need more information.

Important: Some technical records, such as a reserved player ID, may require separate cleanup. We will review these as part of the request and will not claim they have been deleted unless deletion is completed.
