ButtonWorld Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ButtonWorld handles information when you use the game. ButtonWorld is published by CartographeD (“we”, “us” or “our”).

1. Information we handle

  • Player account and identifier: ButtonWorld uses Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous player account. This creates a Firebase user ID used to recognise your account.
  • Player profile and game progress: information associated with your player profile may include your in-game username, scores, progress, virtual currency (such as Sparks or coins), owned or equipped cosmetics, and related game settings.
  • Email address and Google account: if Google sign-in or account linking is enabled and you choose to use it, Google/Firebase may process your email address and account-related information to link or authenticate your account. Playing with an anonymous account does not, by itself, require you to provide an email address.
  • Advertising and device identifiers: ButtonWorld uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The advertising SDK may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising-related information to deliver, limit, measure, and protect advertising, depending on your device settings, consent choices, and the SDK configuration.

2. Information we do not intentionally request

ButtonWorld does not ask you to provide your postal address, phone number, precise GPS location, microphone recordings, photos, or contacts to play the game. Do not include sensitive personal information in your in-game username.

3. Why information is used

  • To create and manage player accounts and, where available, link an account to Google sign-in.
  • To save and synchronise player profiles, scores, progress, virtual currency and cosmetics.
  • To provide, deliver, limit, measure and protect advertising, including rewarded ads where available.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the game and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • To respond to support and account-deletion requests.

4. Services provided by third parties

ButtonWorld uses services from Google/Firebase, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). These providers may process information on our behalf or for their own service purposes, as described in their documentation and policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google advertising information

We do not sell your personal information for money. Depending on the applicable privacy rules and advertising configuration, some advertising-related disclosures to Google or its partners may be considered “sharing”.

5. Data sharing

Information may be processed by Google/Firebase to operate the game's account, database and advertising features. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the service, or to handle a support request. We do not intentionally disclose player profile information to other players unless a feature of the game makes that information visible.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep account and game data for as long as needed to provide the game and for legitimate security or legal purposes. You can request deletion of your ButtonWorld account and associated data by emailing cartographe_games@outlook.com with the subject “ButtonWorld account deletion”. Include your in-game username and do not send your password. We may request limited information to verify the request.

We aim to process verified requests within 30 days. Some records may need to be retained temporarily or where legally required. Certain technical records, such as reserved player IDs, may require separate cleanup; we will not claim that such records have been deleted unless that cleanup has actually been completed.

Account deletion information and instructions: ButtonWorld account deletion page.

7. Security

We use services that provide encryption in transit and apply reasonable measures intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Children

ButtonWorld is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request. The app's target audience and advertising configuration should be checked against the settings selected in Google Play Console.

9. Your choices

You may choose not to link an optional Google account. You can also manage advertising and privacy settings through your device and any consent choices shown in the app, where available. Disabling personalised advertising does not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, contact CartographeD at cartographe_games@outlook.com.

Developer note: This policy must match the exact version of ButtonWorld published on Google Play and the actual SDKs and features enabled in that build. Review the dependencies and Google Play Data safety answers before publishing this page.
